---
name: Media access by plan
description: Product rule for basic image and video access across ZYVIO subscription plans.
---

Basic image and video creation, sending, participation, and viewing must remain available to FREE, PREMIUM, and PREMIUM PRO users. Do not place a “Premium required” gate on basic media use.

**Why:** Photos and videos are a core social feature. Paid plans should be differentiated through higher limits, AI, analytics, personalization, and other enhancements rather than blocking basic media.

**How to apply:** Enforce this rule in posts, profiles, direct messages, Battle, comments, and any future content-creation surface. Media-specific paid features may be added, but selecting, uploading, sending, publishing, and viewing ordinary images or videos must remain available on FREE.
