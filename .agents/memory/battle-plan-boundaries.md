---
name: Battle plan boundaries
description: Product rule separating paid Battle creation limits from unrestricted participation.
---

Subscription limits may restrict how many new Battles a user creates and how many AI generations they request. Joining and voting in Battles created by other users must not be restricted by plan.

**Why:** Paid plans should provide more creation capacity without blocking the core social participation loop for FREE users.

**How to apply:** Keep creation and AI limits in the central plan configuration and enforce them on the backend with persistent daily usage. Do not add plan checks to Battle joining, voting, viewing, or media use.
